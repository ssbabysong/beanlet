Beanlet Support

Beanlet is a small coffee journal: your coffee shelf, a coffee atlas, roast-freshness tips, a pour-over timer and a brew calendar.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/ssbabysong/beanlet/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where are my records saved?
Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account or cloud sync.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?
Go to Atlas → Me → Export backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, use Import backup. Importing adds missing records and never deletes existing ones.

Will deleting the app delete my records?
Yes. Export a backup first.

How do I switch language?
Atlas → Me → choose 中文 or English.

常见问题

记录存在哪里？
只存在你的 iPhone 上的 App 里，没有账号，也没有云同步。

怎么备份、换手机？
「图鉴 → 我的 → 导出备份」，存到「文件」或 iCloud 云盘；新手机上点「导入备份」。导入只会补上缺少的记录，不会删除已有记录。

删掉 App 记录会丢吗？
会，删之前请先导出备份。

怎么切换语言？
「图鉴 → 我的」里选中文或 English。
