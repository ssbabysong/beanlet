# Beanlet App Review submission package

Prepared for App Store Connect after the Guideline 2.1 information request.

## Resolution Center reply

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Beanlet. We have completed quality assurance on a supported physical iPhone and have attached a screen recording captured on a physical device running the latest available iOS. The recording begins with a fresh launch and demonstrates the app's typical user flow.

### 1. Screen recording

The attached recording demonstrates:

- launching Beanlet;
- browsing the built-in coffee bean collection;
- adding a coffee bag to the user's local coffee shelf;
- viewing and updating a bag's remaining amount;
- recording a pour-over or milk coffee, including an optional coffee photo;
- using the guided pour-over timer;
- viewing the brew calendar and monthly/yearly statistics;
- previewing a shareable monthly report and Reel;
- exporting a local backup and changing the interface language.

Beanlet does not have account registration, sign-in, account deletion, paid content, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. It does not host public user-generated content or provide interaction between users, so reporting and blocking mechanisms are not applicable.

### 2. Purpose and target audience

Beanlet is a private coffee journal for home coffee drinkers and specialty-coffee enthusiasts. It helps users keep track of coffee bags, remaining quantities, roast dates and flavor notes; record pour-over and milk-coffee brews; follow a guided pour-over timer; and review their coffee history through a calendar, photo stickers, and monthly or yearly summaries. Its value is keeping these related coffee notes and memories in one simple, visual, bilingual app.

### 3. Access and setup instructions

No account, login credentials, subscription, invitation, special hardware, or sample file is required.

1. Launch the app. It opens directly to the main interface.
2. Open the coffee collection and choose a built-in coffee, then add a bag to the coffee shelf.
3. Open the bag to update its amount or details.
4. Tap the brew action to record a pour-over or milk coffee. Photo access is optional and is requested only after the reviewer chooses to take or select a photo.
5. Open the calendar to view brew history, or open Statistics to view monthly/yearly summaries and sharing previews.
6. The guided pour-over timer is available from the brew flow.
7. Backup export/import and the Chinese/English language selector are available under Bean Atlas > Me.

All records and photos are stored locally on the device. A fresh installation starts with an empty personal shelf, while the built-in coffee collection is immediately available for testing.

### 4. External services, tools, and platforms

Beanlet has no backend, authentication provider, payment processor, advertising network, analytics service, crash-reporting service, cloud database, or runtime AI service.

The app uses these Apple device capabilities only after a user action:

- Camera and Photo Library for optional coffee-bag and cup photos;
- iOS Share Sheet and Files for optional backup export and report/Reel sharing;
- device motion for the optional sticker movement effect;
- haptics for light interaction feedback.

The built-in coffee collection contains factual coffee and roaster references. Source and brewing-guide web pages open in the browser only when the user taps a link. The app does not fetch remote content automatically for its core functionality.

### 5. Regional differences

Beanlet functions consistently in all regions. There are no location-based restrictions, region-specific accounts, payments, or feature differences. Users may choose Chinese or English; this changes interface and built-in catalog text only.

### 6. Regulated services and third-party material

Beanlet is a personal coffee journal and does not operate in a regulated industry. It does not provide health, financial, gambling, legal, or other regulated services.

The app does not include third-party product photography, package artwork, or brand logos. Built-in coffee covers are original Beanlet watercolor illustrations supplied as static app assets. Product and roaster names are used only as factual catalog references, with source links where applicable. Bundled fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. No external AI service is called by the app at runtime.

Please let us know if any additional information is required. Thank you.

## Pre-submission QA checklist

- Install the final Release/TestFlight build on a physical supported iPhone.
- Confirm the device runs the latest available iOS.
- Test a fresh install and an upgrade install.
- Test denying and later granting Camera, Photos, and Motion permissions.
- Add, edit, finish, and delete a coffee bag.
- Record, edit, and delete both a pour-over and milk coffee.
- Verify used-up bags cannot be selected for a new brew.
- Exercise the timer, calendar, month/year statistics, monthly/annual report preview, Reel preview/export, and backup export/import.
- Force-quit and relaunch; verify local records remain.
- Test in both Chinese and English.
- Verify the App Store screenshots show the actual app UI.
- Confirm the Privacy Policy and Support URLs are publicly reachable.
- Increment the iOS build number above the rejected build before uploading the new binary.
