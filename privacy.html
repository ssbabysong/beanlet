Beanlet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version

Beanlet does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you record stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your coffee shelf, brew records, tasting notes, stickers and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone.
  • Photos you take or choose for a coffee bag or a brew are saved only inside the app on your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The developer cannot see your records.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: only used when you choose to take or pick a photo of a coffee bag or a cup.
  • Motion: only used on the sticker page, so a gentle shake makes your stickers tumble. Motion data never leaves your device.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share a monthly recap, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, iCloud Drive, Messages and so on) is up to you.

No tracking, ads or analytics

Beanlet has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not connect to the internet on its own. Links to roasters' and brewing guides' websites open only when you tap them.

Children

Beanlet does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/ssbabysong/beanlet/issues.

Beanlet 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

简单说

Beanlet 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。你记录的一切都只保存在你自己的设备上。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 咖啡豆架、冲煮记录、风味笔记、贴纸和设置，只保存在这台 iPhone 上 App 自己的存储里。
  • 你为豆袋或咖啡拍摄、选择的照片，只保存在设备上的 App 里。
  • 没有账号、不用登录、没有服务器，开发者看不到你的记录。

权限

  • 相机和照片：只在你主动拍摄或选择豆袋、咖啡照片时使用。
  • 运动与健身：只在贴纸页使用，轻轻晃动手机让贴纸碰撞。数据不会离开你的设备。

备份与分享

导出备份或分享月度回顾时，App 会生成一个文件交给 iOS 分享菜单，存到哪里由你决定。

没有追踪、广告或统计

Beanlet 不含任何广告、统计、崩溃上报或追踪 SDK，也不会自己联网。只有你点击时，才会打开烘焙商或冲煮指南的网页。

联系

有问题请在 github.com/ssbabysong/beanlet/issues 留言。
