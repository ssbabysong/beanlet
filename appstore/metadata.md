## 给审核员的备注（App Review Information → Notes）

No account or login is required, and the app works fully offline. All records and photos are stored only on the device (IndexedDB inside the app). To try it quickly: open the third tab (atlas), tap "Coffee library", pick any coffee and add it to your shelf; then open it from the first tab and tap "Log a brew". The second tab shows the brew calendar; tap the month summary above it for the monthly recap. Camera and photo access are only requested when the user chooses to add a photo of a coffee bag or a cup. Motion access is only used on the sticker page so a gentle shake makes the stickers tumble.

Built-in coffee covers are original watercolor illustrations created for Beanlet. Roaster and coffee names are listed only as factual product information with links to the roasters' own pages; Beanlet is not affiliated with these roasters.
