## 给审核员的备注（App Review Information → Notes）

No account or login is required, and the app works fully offline. Records and photos are stored on the device and, when the user is signed in to iCloud, synced to their own private iCloud database (CloudKit); the developer has no server and cannot see this data. To try it quickly: open the third tab, tap "Coffee library", pick any coffee and add it to your shelf; then open it from the first tab and tap "Log a brew" (in the top bar). The second tab shows the brew calendar; tap the month entry above it to open that month's journal, and turn pages by swiping or tapping either side. Camera and photo access are only requested when the user chooses to add a photo of a coffee bag or a cup.

Built-in coffee covers are original watercolor illustrations created for Beanlet. Roaster and coffee names are listed only as factual product information with links to the roasters' own pages; Beanlet is not affiliated with these roasters.
